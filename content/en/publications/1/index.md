On February 14, I attended a lecture by Stanislav Vladimirovich Drobyshevsky, a leading Russian anthropologist. The lecture's topic was ["How to survive the apocalypse"](https://mediomodo.ru/). It took place in a real bunker at a depth of 65 meters underground. [Bunker-42](https://bunker42.com/) is located in Moscow on Taganka Street and now houses a Cold War museum.
