Last week, I attended a lecture on pixel art video games. The lecture was led by the authors of the blog "Escapism on the Edge." We discussed how people, feeling nostalgia, return to these games and play them, despite their hopeless age. The lecture mentioned games like Super Mario Bros., Castlevania, and Metroid. Remarkably, speedrunning Super Mario is still a competitive eSport, with results even appearing in the Guinness Book of World Records. I've always found it fascinating to learn new facts about the development and legacy of old games.
