I recently visited the [Yandex Museum](https://museum.yandex.ru/) for the second time. A truly atmospheric place for those who want to step back in time to the 80s. Lots of old computers and consoles, including the iMac G3, Commodore 64, and Super Famicom. All the equipment is working, so you can sit back and play old games. You can also have coffee, read books about games from the library, and choose a souvenir: a T-shirt, a mug, a book, or a magnet. Admission is free.
